1. Overview

This Privacy Policy applies to Qingdu (청독), a Chinese character reader, and Seidoku, a Japanese reader (together, "the Apps"), developed by Qingdu Studio. The Apps recognize text through your camera and help you read it with pronunciation and translation. This policy explains how information is handled when you use the Apps.

2. Information We Do Not Collect

  • We do not require account creation or login
  • We do not collect your name, email address, or any personal identifiers
  • We do not store images or photos you take within the Apps
  • We do not sell or share your data with third parties for advertising purposes

3. Camera and Gallery Access

The Apps request access to your device's camera and photo gallery solely to capture images containing Chinese or Japanese characters for OCR (Optical Character Recognition) processing. Images are processed in real time and are not stored on our servers.

4. Third-Party Services

The Apps use the following third-party services. Images and extracted text may be transmitted to Google's servers for processing via the Gemini API.

The Apps do not retain any user data. All processing is done in real time and results are displayed only within the current session.

6. Children's Privacy

The Apps are suitable for all ages. We do not knowingly collect any personal information from children under the age of 13.

7. Changes to This Policy
